The Cut: the agent never quotes a price

The first draft had the voice agent "provide indicative pricing" from pricing.md. That is removed. A price said in the studio's voice is heard as a commitment and cannot be taken back. pricing.md is never loaded into any prompt, never committed and never bundled. A code-level guard strips any price line from AI output, and a caller who asks about price is still qualified normally.
